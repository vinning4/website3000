// About Me

I'm Vin, a web developer based in Melbourne AU. I enjoy working with business owners and fixing a common problem: underperforming websites.

With a background in bioengineering and product design, I take a function-first approach: if it doesn't help your customers, it doesn't belong on your site.

// About Websites

Too many business websites are built once and forgotten. They're slow on mobile, invisible to Google, designed to simply show existence. Over time, they become a liability, casting doubt on an otherwise honest business.

The standard web design options also fall short. On one end are low-cost, template-driven WordPress builds that fail at the foundational mechanics. On the other are high-dollar agencies encumbered by process, overhead, and jargon. Both approaches miss the point.

An effective website functions as a commercial instrument. It helps the right audience find you, understand your offer quickly, and act. For most businesses, this requires just 3 to 5 fast, well-structured pages.

Then there's the DIY path. While anyone can publish a site, building something that actually performs requires precise execution — to attract qualified visitors, load instantly, and rank on Google. Done right, your website becomes a productive digital storefront.

// The System

Website3000 is different — an alternative to the standard mess. Every site is hand-coded, meaning I don't use off-the-shelf templates, page builders or heavy frameworks. I write every line of code in semantic HTML, CSS and JavaScript. This requires technical depth, can be tedious, and is therefore an uncommon approach. The end result is a simpler, more deterministic system that's bulletproof: no bloated layers, no databases to hack, and no plugins to crash your site next month. Just a lean, clean website engineered to load fast, capture enquiries, and render perfectly on any device.

This engineered simplicity extends to how the site is maintained. The subscription model exists because a website should remain a worry-free utility as your business grows. Fast content updates, secure hosting, and performance monitoring are included — giving you an on-call developer to make sure things run smoothly.
