// About Me

I'm Vin, a web developer in Melbourne AU. I enjoy helping businesses fix a costly problem: websites that don't perform.

My background is in bioengineering and product design, which informs a clinical, function-first approach: if it doesn't help your customers, it doesn't belong on your site.

I'm independent by design, and selective. I partner with business owners who see a website as a long-term investment, not just a digital brochure. It also means you work directly with me—no account managers, no call queues, no jargon.

// About Websites

Too many business websites are built once and forgotten. They're slow on mobile, invisible to Google, and designed to simply exist. Over time, they become a liability, casting doubt on an otherwise honest business.

The standard options also fall short. Low-cost WordPress templates fail at the foundational mechanics. Big agencies overwhelm you with cost and process. Both approaches miss the point.

An effective website is a commercial instrument: it helps the right audience find you, understand your offer quickly, and act. This means optimising for search, using content silos, and designing friction-free pathways. For most businesses, this requires just 3 to 5 fast, well-structured pages.

// The System

Website3000 is an alternative to the standard mess. Every site is hand-coded. I don't use off-the-shelf templates or page builders. Writing every line from scratch in pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript is a slow, meticulous way to build—and that is exactly why it works. The result is a secure, deterministic system: no bloated layers, no databases to hack, and no plugins to crash your site next month. Just fast sites built for search and conversion.

This simplicity extends to site maintenance. The monthly subscription keeps your site a worry-free utility as your business grows. Edits, hosting and performance monitoring are all handled for you by an on-call developer.
